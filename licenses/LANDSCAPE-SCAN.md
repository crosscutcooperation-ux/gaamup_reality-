# Downloaded landscape

**Photogrammetry | 3D scan nature landscape**, by **Laslo.S**.

- [Original model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/photogrammetry-3d-scan-nature-landscape-e0050374e75347929017942fe761670f)
- [Creator](https://sketchfab.com/Laslo.S)
- [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The user supplied the downloaded GLB from their Downloads folder. Its embedded author/license metadata and the official model metadata identify the same source and license. The source file was preserved unchanged.

Changes: source transforms applied; recentered and uniformly scaled to a 260-unit landscape; a narrow railway corridor graded to the sampled scan surface; geometry reduced to about 585,000 triangles while retaining the supplied texture maps and material boundaries. Railway, platforms and train are separate scene additions. Destination names are illustrative navigation controls, not geographic labels for the scanned site.

Attribution is visible beneath the map and embedded in the exported GLB. Source/output SHA-256 values, bounds and modifications are in `public/assets/landscape/manifest.json`.
